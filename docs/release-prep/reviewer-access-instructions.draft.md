## Factual English navigation text

No registration or login is required. Open Settings to find Remove Ads,
Charts Pro and Favorites Pro. Core currency conversion is free. Purchases use
Google Play Billing. Restore purchases restores entitlements already owned by
the same Google Play account. Charts Pro and Favorites Pro unlock the features
described in Settings. There are no app accounts or subscriptions.

This text explains navigation/purchases, but does not supply free access to
unowned paid features. Therefore it cannot justify checking the Console box
that the supplied instructions provide full access including paid content.
Keep this distinction visible in the final Production review. Closed approval
alone is not separate confirmation of how reviewers obtain paid entitlements.
