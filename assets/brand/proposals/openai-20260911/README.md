# OpenAI icon exploration — 2026-09-11

These PNGs are selection references generated with the built-in OpenAI image
tool. They are not production assets and are not consumed by the application.

The shared brief asked for a quiet, vector-friendly Currency Converter mark
that combines exchange motion with Honest Fern's botanical geometry and palette.
Every prompt prohibited text, currency symbols, coin rims, charts, financial
growth imagery, shadows, texture, and small details. The Honest Fern split-pod
mark was supplied as a brand reference, not an edit target.

Files:

- `concept-a-circular-fronds.png` — two fronds forming a circular exchange loop;
- `concept-b-botanical-swap.png` — a botanical ribbon forming opposing motion;
- `concept-c-split-pod-exchange.png` — mirrored pod halves with central
  directional negative space;
- `concept-c2-split-pod-refined.png` — larger targeted refinement of C;
- `concept-d-opposing-leaf-directions.png` — horizontal opposing leaf forms.
- `concept-e-gold-orbit-hybrid.png` — OpenAI refinement of MiniMax M3: clearer
  gold coin and circular exchange, but the center still reads like a coffee
  bean and the result resembles a crypto token.
- `concept-f-modern-gold-swap.png` — revised coin-first direction with two
  separate horizontal arrows and no botanical object; current recommendation.

Generated platform-shaped corners and uncontrolled raster shading are concept
artifacts. Luis selected F. Its production version is rebuilt from exact vector
geometry with one restrained, deterministic gold gradient, then regenerated
through `scripts/generate_brand_icons.sh`.
